01 / THE CHALLENGE

Decisions without
the whole picture.

Battlecode is not just a movement problem. A bot has to make its next choice before knowing everything ahead of it. BigHero approached this through observable information, cautious memory, graph-based routes and tactical evaluation.

01 / PERCEPTION

See what is known.

Observe visible terrain and current positions while keeping unknown areas explicitly uncertain.

02 / NAVIGATION

Find viable routes.

Evaluate accessible moves, spatial constraints, obstacles and potential collision risks.

03 / ADAPTATION

Choose for the moment.

Balance survival, exploration, resources and tactical opportunities as conditions change.

03 / TACTICAL SYSTEMS

Built to do more
than just move.

The bot combines several smaller decision rules rather than relying on one fixed path or one simplistic objective. Each module helps solve a different uncertainty.

Graph-based exploration

Build traversable connections from observed terrain, evaluate reachable regions, and account for the boundary between known and unknown space.

Risk-aware positioning

Consider collisions, constrained spaces and dangerous routes when weighing potential actions and safe alternatives.

Portal & resource memory

Use remembered observations to inform later moves without treating unseen portal exits or outdated information as confirmed safe.

Adaptive unit decisions

Apply tactical movement and splitting rules while protecting important units and managing changing game conditions.

05 / BUILD PROCESS

Test. Learn.
Refine.

BigHero emerged from multiple strategy iterations. The development archive contains experimental approaches, replay-based comparisons, audit scripts and notes on rejected ideas.

FOUNDATION
