# BigHero | UNSW Battlecode 2026

**Python Strategy Bot | Team of 2 | Peak Leaderboard Rank:126**

## Overview

BigHero is an autonomous Python strategy bot developed for UNSW Battlecode 2026.

Our goal was to build a bot that could make intelligent tactical decisions in a competitive environment with limited visibility. Throughout the competition, we developed multiple versions of BigHero, experimenting with different algorithms and strategies to improve its decision-making capabilities.

## Key Technical Features

**Graph-Based Navigation**

Represented accessible terrain as a graph to evaluate movement options, identify viable routes and navigate complex environments.

**Heuristic Decision-Making**

Developed a scoring approach that considers available space, potential threats, resources and collision risks when selecting actions.

**Enemy Threat Assessment**

Evaluated potential enemy movements and dangerous positions to support safer tactical decisions.

**Adaptive Strategy and Unit Management**

Implemented tactical movement, dynamic splitting, role-based behaviour and defensive decisions that respond to changing game conditions.

**Memory and Exploration**

Used stored information about explored terrain, resources and portal connections to support decision-making under partial visibility.

## Development and Testing

BigHero evolved through multiple versions, each involving strategy changes, debugging and repeated evaluation.

Our development process included:

- Implementing and refining Python algorithms.
- Experimenting with alternative strategies and decision-making rules.
- Analysing gameplay replays to identify weaknesses.
- Testing tactical behaviour across different maps and scenarios.
- Reviewing performance and execution constraints before finalising the bot.
